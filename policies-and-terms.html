Site Policies and Terms:

Last updated: 12/19/2024

Introduction:

Welcome to my personal website! Thank you for visiting. This document explains how your access to the site is managed in terms of privacy, cookies, security, and terms of use. I value your privacy and want to be transparent about how information is collected and used during your visit.

1. Data Collection and Use:

I use a few tools to better understand how my site is accessed and improve the visitor experience. Google Analytics collects basic data such as pages visited and time spent on the site, always anonymously. I also use Google Tag Manager to efficiently manage these analytics tools.

The collected data helps me understand which content is most interesting and how I can improve the site. I do not access personally identifiable information and do not share your data with third parties beyond the tools necessary for the site’s operation (such as Google Analytics itself).

2. Cookies:

My site uses cookies to function properly. Essential cookies ensure that basic site functions work correctly. Google Analytics uses its own cookies to generate anonymous access statistics.

If you prefer not to be tracked, you can disable cookies in your browser or use the Google Analytics opt-out extension. I understand that privacy is important, so I maintain only the minimum necessary cookies for the site to function well.

You can control and manage cookies in the following ways:

Browser Settings: You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings. Each browser provides tools for managing cookies:

- Chrome: Manage cookies

- Firefox: Manage cookies

- Edge: Manage cookies

- Safari: Manage cookies

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain parts of this site.

3. Security Measures:

I have implemented various measures to ensure safe browsing:

- The site uses HTTPS with a GitHub digital certificate;

- If you know how to configure, use proxies and DNS servers;

- You can verify the site’s security at any time using VirusTotal (available here);

- The site is compatible with VPN and private networks like Tor Browser for those seeking more privacy;

- I use randomized emails through SimpleLogin for secure communications.

For your own security, I recommend keeping your browser updated and, if possible, using a VPN when browsing the internet.

4. Terms of Use:

This is a personal website intended for sharing knowledge and information. All content published here is either my own or properly credited when from third parties. You are welcome to read and share the content, but I ask that you respect copyright and cite the source when using any material.

The site may contain links to other websites and external resources. I have no control over the content of these sites and am not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

5. Your Rights:

You have full control over your data while browsing the site. You can choose to:

- Disable Google Analytics tracking;

- Control cookies through your browser settings;

- Use privacy tools such as proxies and DNS servers, VPN or Tor;

- Request information about collected data by contacting me.

6. Policy Updates:

This document may be occasionally updated to reflect changes to the site or regulations. I recommend checking it periodically. I maintain a commitment to transparency and visitor privacy, so any significant changes will be highlighted.

All content presented here aims to create a safe and pleasant environment for sharing knowledge and information. If you have any questions or suggestions about these policies, feel free to contacting me.
