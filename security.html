Security

In the digital world, it's crucial to know that not everything on the internet is secure, but I strive to make sure your browsing experience is as safe as possible. Here are some measures I've taken to protect everyone visiting this site:

1. HTTPS and GitHub Digital Certificate:

This site uses HTTPS, meaning the connection between you and this site is encrypted. Additionally, the digital certificate is issued by GitHub, which helps ensure that communication is secure and protected against man-in-the-middle attacks.

2. Security Scanning with VirusTotal:

To ensure the site is free from threats, you can check the security of my site at any time using VirusTotal:

Scan site with VirusTotal.

This allows you to get a detailed view of the security status of my page.

3. Google Analytics Opt-out and Cookie Control:

I respect your privacy. If you prefer not to have your online behavior tracked, you can opt-out of Google Analytics. Additionally, you can disable cookies directly in your browser.

4. Use of Proxies and DNS Servers:

For added anonymity and security, you can use proxies or DNS servers that provide extra layers of protection and privacy. This helps protect your identity and browsing activities.

5. VPN (Virtual Private Network):

A VPN is an excellent way to protect your online privacy by creating an encrypted connection between you and the internet. It helps mask your location and ensures your online activities are more secure.

6. Anonymous Browsing with Tor Browser:

If you want a higher level of anonymity, you can use Tor Browser, which allows you to browse the web anonymously, hiding your IP and making it harder to track your online activities.

7. Randomized Email with SimpleLogin:

To enhance privacy, I use randomly generated emails with the SimpleLogin

service on my contact page. This helps protect my real email and keeps communication secure and private.

What You Can Do to Ensure Your Own Online Security?

- Keep your antivirus updated. Protect yourself from threats and malware.

- Be cautious with unknown links. Always check the URL before clicking!

- Consider using multi-factor authentication (2FA) whenever available.
