Privacy Policy

Last updated: 05/11/2025

This Privacy Policy describes how user data is handled on the sites Enzo Fuke, hosted on the domains milesonerd.github.io and milesonerd.netlify.app.

1. Scope

This policy applies to both sites, which are managed and maintained by Enzo Fuke. Each site may have specific features and practices as described below.

2. Data Collected

2.1 Automatically Collected Data

  • The site hosted on GitHub Pages (milesonerd.github.io) uses Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, which may collect data such as:
    • Approximate location (based on IP),
    • Pages visited,
    • Time spent on the site,
    • Device and browser type.
  • The site hosted on Netlify (milesonerd.netlify.app) does not collect data automatically and does not use cookies.

2.2 Contact Form

Both sites offer an optional contact form where users can enter their email address. The data is sent through the Formspree service, which requires a Google reCAPTCHA verification.

  • Filling out the form is optional.
  • The email address is used only to respond to the submitted message.
  • Messages received are read and subsequently manually deleted.

3. Cookies

  • The site hosted on GitHub Pages may use cookies through Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager.
  • The site hosted on Netlify does not use its own cookies or third-party cookies.

4. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services and CDNs are used on the sites:

  • Formspree – Form processing service;
  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager – Usage analysis (only on GitHub Pages);
  • Public CDNs – Used for libraries and styling:
    • Showdown (Markdown rendering) used on both sites;
    • Tailwind CSS (styling), Font Awesome, and SimpleIcons (icons) used only on the Netlify site.

The privacy practices of these services are subject to their own policies.

5. Data Sharing

No collected data is shared with third parties, except as required by law or through the tools used for analysis and forms, such as Google and Formspree.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Messages received through the contact form are deleted after being read. No backups of this data are made.

7. User Rights

Users residing in the European Union or other jurisdictions with specific data protection laws have the right to:

  • Access the data they have provided,
  • Correct any incorrect information,
  • Request the deletion of their data.

To exercise these rights, please contact through the available contact form on the sites.

8. Security

Technical and organizational measures have been adopted to protect transmitted data, including the use of HTTPS and reCAPTCHA verification on the forms.

9. Disclaimer

Use of the sites is at the user's own risk. The site administrator disclaims any liability arising from the use or interpretation of the content.

10. Changes to the Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated at any time. It is recommended to review it periodically.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the contact form available on both sites.
