Cookie Policy

Last updated: 05/13/2025

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies are used on the sites Enzo Fuke, hosted on the domains milesonerd.github.io and milesonerd.netlify.app.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to help enhance the user experience by storing preferences, session data, and enabling website features.

2. Types of Cookies Used

There are two main types of cookies used on the sites:

  • Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the basic functionality of the website, such as ensuring the proper functioning of forms (e.g., Formspree) and preventing spam (e.g., Google reCAPTCHA).
  • Analytics Cookies: These cookies are used for tracking and analyzing website usage to improve performance. Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager are used on the GitHub Pages site (milesonerd.github.io) to collect anonymous data about how visitors interact with the site, such as:
    • Pages visited
    • Time spent on the site
    • Approximate location based on IP
    • Device and browser type

3. Cookies on the Sites

3.1 Site on GitHub Pages (milesonerd.github.io)

The GitHub Pages site uses cookies for:

  • Google Analytics – For analyzing website traffic and user behavior.
  • Google Tag Manager – For managing and triggering marketing tags.

3. Cookies

  • The site hosted on GitHub Pages may use cookies through Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager.
  • The site hosted on Netlify does not use its own cookies or third-party cookies.

3.2 Site on Netlify (milesonerd.netlify.app))

The Netlify site does not use cookies.

The privacy practices of these services are subject to their own policies.

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting cookies. However, please note that blocking or deleting cookies may impact the functionality of the websites, including the contact forms and certain interactive features.

5. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to cookies used by the websites, third-party services such as Formspree (for form submissions) and Google Analytics may place cookies on your device. Please review the cookie policies of these third-party services for further details:

  • Google Analytics Cookie Policy
  • Formspree Privacy Policy

6. Changes to the Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated periodically to reflect any changes in the cookies we use or the services we implement. We recommend reviewing this policy regularly for updates.

7. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Cookie Policy, please feel free to contact us through the contact form available on both websites.
