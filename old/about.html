About Me

I am a Data and Machine Learning Professional, as well as a Full Stack Developer with expertise in a wide array of programming languages. I work with Python, SQL, Go, C#, R, Assembly, Rust, Solidity, Ruby, Kotlin, TypeScript, C++, C, and JavaScript, as well as NoSQL databases. Additionally, I am skilled in markup languages such as Markdown, HTML, and CSS, and scripting languages including Shell Script, GNU Bash and LUA, and the JSON data format. But I mainly focus on Python, Go, C, SQL and C#.

My passion for technology is driven by my deep interest in data, Artificial Intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity, fields in which I am constantly expanding my knowledge. I am a firm believer in the power of decentralized systems and advocate for a more decentralized internet, where users have more control over their data and online experiences. I love Open Source and Linux.

With a strong foundation in both the technical and ethical aspects of technology, I strive to create solutions that not only advance innovation but also promote privacy, security, and freedom. I am always learning, exploring new ideas, and working to make a meaningful impact in the world of technology.
